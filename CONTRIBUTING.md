# Contributing to AuthHero

Thanks for contributing! Two things to know before your first PR:

## 1. Licensing model

AuthHero is **dual-licensed** — AGPL-3.0-only plus a commercial license (see
[LICENSING.md](./LICENSING.md)). A few integration-surface packages
(`@authhero/adapter-interfaces`, `create-authhero`, `@authhero/widget`) are MIT.

## 2. Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

To keep the dual-licensing model possible, every contributor signs the
[CLA](./CLA.md) once. It grants the maintainer the right to distribute your
contribution under both licenses — you keep full ownership of your work.

The CLA bot will prompt you on your first pull request; signing is a single PR
comment:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

## Development

See the [README](./README.md) for setup, commands, and the changeset workflow
(every PR that modifies a versioned package should include a changeset —
`pnpm changeset`).
